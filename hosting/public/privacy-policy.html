Privacy Policy

This policy explains how information is handled when you use the Meat Station mobile application.

Version 1.0.0 · Last updated 09 October 2026 · Meat Station Mobile Application

Information we collect

Account and profile

We use your name, mobile number, authentication information, account identifier and account status to create and protect your customer account.

Delivery addresses and location

Saved addresses may contain the recipient name, phone number, address, landmark, address label and a map location pin. Location permission is used when you ask the app to locate or select a delivery destination. The app does not require continuous background location tracking.

Orders and fulfilment

Order records may include products, quantities, prices, totals, delivery slots, delivery instructions, status history and issue reports.

Payments

We record the selected payment method, amount, payment status and provider references when applicable. Sensitive card or UPI credentials are handled by the configured payment provider and are not stored by Meat Station.

Devices, notifications and diagnostics

Push-notification tokens, application interactions, device identifiers, crash reports and diagnostic information may be processed to operate, secure and improve the app.

Images and content

Authorized store personnel may upload product, category and promotional images. Customers may provide delivery instructions or information in support and order-issue requests.

How information is used

  • Authenticate users and manage accounts.
  • Display products, calculate carts and process orders.
  • Prepare and deliver orders to the selected address.
  • Provide order, payment and operational notifications.
  • Respond to support and order-issue requests.
  • Protect accounts, prevent fraud and diagnose failures.
  • Understand application performance and improve the service.

Service providers and access

Information is processed using configured cloud hosting, authentication, database, storage, notification, analytics, crash-reporting and mapping services. Assigned administrators, staff and delivery personnel receive only the customer and order information required for their authorized duties. Information may also be disclosed when legally required or necessary to protect users and the service.

We do not sell customers' personal information.

Security and retention

Application traffic uses encrypted connections, and access to operational records is controlled using authenticated roles and permissions. No system can guarantee absolute security.

Account and operational information is retained while needed to provide the service. When an eligible account is deleted, its profile, saved addresses, cart, notifications, device registrations and sign-in identity are removed. Historical order and payment facts may be retained in anonymized or legally required form for accounting, tax, fraud-prevention, dispute and security purposes.

Your choices

You can update profile information, manage saved addresses and notifications, control device permissions, and request account deletion. See the account deletion page for both in-app and external request options.

Children

The Meat Station service is intended for adults and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly create accounts for children.

Policy updates and contact

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The revision date above identifies the current version.

For privacy questions, contact stsmeatstation@gmail.com.
