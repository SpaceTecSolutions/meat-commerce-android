Delete your account

Customers can permanently delete an eligible Meat Station account in the app or request deletion when app access is unavailable.

These instructions apply to the Meat StationAndroid mobile application

Delete your account in the app

  1. Sign in to the Meat Station application.
  2. Open Profile.
  3. Open Profile Information or Delete Account.
  4. Review the data-removal notice and confirm Delete My Account.

If an order is still active, deletion remains unavailable until that order is delivered or cancelled. This protects the information needed to complete or resolve the order.

Request deletion without app access

Send a request from the email account available to you. Include the mobile number registered to the Meat Station account and write Delete my Meat Station account. Do not send an OTP, password, payment credential or identity document in the initial email.

Email deletion request

Support may request a limited verification step to prevent another person from deleting your account. Any active order must be completed or cancelled before deletion can be finalized.

Data that is deleted

  • Customer profile and sign-in identity.
  • Saved delivery addresses and map pins.
  • Shopping cart and temporary checkout selections.
  • In-app notifications and registered push-notification devices.
  • Personal identifiers connected to retained historical records, where deletion is not restricted by law.

Data that may be retained

Historical order and payment facts may be retained without your name, phone number, delivery address or account identifier when required for accounting, tax, legal compliance, fraud prevention, security or dispute resolution. The request and verification correspondence may be retained as evidence that the deletion request was completed.

Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.
